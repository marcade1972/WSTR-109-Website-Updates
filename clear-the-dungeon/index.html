How to play
  1. The twelve face cards are the monsters, dealt into four lairs of three. Only the top monster of each lair can be fought. Jacks have 11 power, Queens 12, Kings 13.
  2. The rest of the deck is your power deck: Aces count 1, number cards their value, and Jokers count 10 and match any suit.
  3. Each turn you draw three cards. Pick a card, then pick a lair to play it on that monster.
  4. A monster takes exactly three cards. The first two are strikes, and together they must total at least the monster's power. The third is the finishing blow and must match the monster's suit. Its rank does not matter.
  5. Cards you play stay on the monster between turns. When the finishing blow lands, the monster is slain and the next one in the lair is revealed.
  6. When you end your turn, any cards left in your hand go to the damage bar. If it fills to seven cards, the dungeon wins.
  7. Reserve rule: once per turn you may play the top card of the damage bar as if it were in your hand.
  8. Slay all twelve monsters to win. Your score is the number of cards still in the power deck.

Clear the Dungeon was designed by Mark S. Ball. You can undo any play within the current turn.
