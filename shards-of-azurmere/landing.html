The world

One village. Four dungeons. Dozens of rooms.

Start in Wrenwick, then pick your way through a mossy crypt, a flooded tower and a cavern of lava before you can break into the Azure Keep. Every dungeon has its own tool to find, its own puzzles and its own boss.

How it plays

Roll a handful of d4s. Keep the highest.

When you act under pressure you roll one four-sided die for each point in the attribute that fits, Power, Nerve or Wits, and only your best die counts. One roll decides what happens to you and the monster.

  • 1–2You fail. In a fight, you lose a heart.
  • 3You succeed, but it costs you a heart (unless you were defending).
  • 4A clean success. Every extra 4 is an extra hit.

Your hero

Fill in your hero sheet

Choose a kin, split 3, 2 and 1 between your attributes, pick a starting item (lantern, bow, boomerang or magic cape) and your colours. Every kin plays differently.

  • SYLPHFocus for an extra die.
  • SKYLINGGlide over pits and lava.
  • STONEBORNShrug off a lost heart.
  • TIDEKINSwim deep water.
  • SPRIGLINGVanish out of a fight.
  • GLITCHLINGReroll a bad die.
